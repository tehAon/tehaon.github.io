Fuel On Demand

Burn Fuel Only While Working

Forges, campfires and chem stations burn fuel only while they are actually working. No overburn on the last item, the fire goes out when the craft queue empties, and a jammed station goes dark until you make room. Leftover fuel is kept. Empty stations still burn for heat. Server-side only; players install nothing.
